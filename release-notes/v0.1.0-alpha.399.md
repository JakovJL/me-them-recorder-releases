# Me/Them Recorder 0.1.0-alpha.399

Changes since **0.1.0-alpha.397**.

## New

- Meet auto-record (off by default): join a Google Meet call in your browser and recording starts; leave and it stops. A new meeting asks whether to record always, only now or never, and remembers the answer for the whole series.
- A Meet strip stays over every screen: discard the first 30 seconds if the call was not meant to be recorded, and get a Windows notification with a short sound when a recording starts while the window is hidden.
- No setup wizard: the record disc shows the microphone level and whether the next recording can be transcribed. If a speech model or a Groq key is missing, it offers the one download or key right there.
- Features that need an AI key ask for it where they are used, and a key can be saved during a recording. New users see a one-time note that closing to the tray keeps the app running.
- A fresh install starts on the recommended speech model instead of asking you to choose one.

## Fixed

- The setup download now appears in one place on the recorder, with a single progress indicator.
- The Meet status stays aligned, and the disc's input level comes back after switching devices or hiding and restoring the window.

## Important

- Meet auto-record is off by default. Turn it on in Settings → Meet.
- It uses no Google account, calendar or network: the app only sees that the browser holds the microphone and which Meet tab is open.
- Meet auto-record was checked in Edge from the source build. The installed build, Chrome/Firefox, Interview meetings with a saved preparation profile and the "always / never" rules have not yet been verified in a real call.
- The old first-run wizard and Settings → "Run setup again" are gone; the record disc and the readiness row now offer the same setup steps.
