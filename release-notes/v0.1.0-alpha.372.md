# Me/Them Recorder 0.1.0-alpha.372

Changes since **0.1.0-alpha.363**.

## New

- Keep a separate Interview index for each position while reusing existing notes inside the same Obsidian vault.
- Prepare Interview answers from a local notes folder; Quick, Full and Retry share one file selection.
- Check the folder in Preparation, see broken links and oversized files, and open help for the index format.
- Choose a reasoning level from Off to High for Quick and Full; answers avoid examples you already used.

## Fixed

- Full adds a new example after Quick without repeating its answer.
- Interview reasoning settings survive a restart, and Full works with more AI providers.
- Interview answers stay closer to the question and avoid inventing prices, plans or exact numbers your preparation does not contain; questions about your own work also use your personal notes.

## Important

- Create a UTF-8 `index.md` with relative links such as `- [project and questions it answers](project.md)`; supported notes are `.md` and `.txt`.
- Limits: 32,000 characters and 200 entries in the index, 15,000 characters per file, up to three whole files per answer; oversized files are skipped.
- Knowledge selection adds an AI request before each answer; a failed check or selection leaves the preparation fields available.
- Leave the knowledge folder empty to keep using your existing preparation; folder and preparation changes during recording apply to the next session.
- On computers with performance and efficiency cores, live transcription now prefers the performance cores to reduce delay. If live text still lags, copy the session log with the log button in the top bar and send it.
- Interview and Ghost still need full real-call and conferencing acceptance.
- A recording may be missing from history after transcription is cancelled.
- System-audio timing can become misaligned and change the order of transcript lines.
