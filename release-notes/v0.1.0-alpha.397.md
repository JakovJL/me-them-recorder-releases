# Me/Them Recorder 0.1.0-alpha.397

Changes since **0.1.0-alpha.384**.

## New

- An open meeting is one workspace: the transcript stays on screen beside the summary, questions and participation. It reads as replies with chapters, marks decisions and tasks, and follows the sound phrase by phrase while you listen.
- The player is a map of the meeting: who spoke when, the chapters, the decisions and tasks. Point at it to see what was said there; click to listen from that moment. Participation compares the speakers side by side and shows who held the floor in each chapter.
- The Record button is now a conversation disc. Its centre starts and stops the recording; around it a needle draws who spoke when. The whole meeting always fits one turn, and every meeting in History has its own small disc.
- Sound check before a call: rest the pointer on the centre of the disc to see the live sound of your microphone and of the system sound. Nothing is recorded.
- Preparation profiles: keep several complete preparations (resume, vacancy, rules, knowledge base, check questions) and switch between them at the top of the preparation window.
- Preparation has a Check section: it answers sample questions with what is in the window now, saved or not, and keeps the previous answers beside the new ones so you can see what your edit changed.
- The preparation window is larger and calmer: one style for every section, the knowledge base in its own section, the Quick and Full texts shown whole, and one Save for everything, providers included.
- New answer format "By the question": a short reply to a simple question, the answer and a few points for an explanation, a story for experience. Spoken points stays the default.
- Personal rules have a choice for experience your preparation does not mention: say how you do it, or answer as your own experience.
- Interview answers rely only on what was really said in the conversation. A story you have already told is not offered again.

## Fixed

- After the Ghost window is closed, the main window can be moved by its title bar again.
- A text field you click into has one highlighted frame instead of two.
- Answers no longer open with "I don't have experience with…" when your preparation does not mention a tool.

## Important

- While the pointer rests on the disc, Windows shows the microphone as in use. That is the sound check; it stops a few seconds after you move away.
- Your current preparation becomes the profile "Main preparation". The Answer style section is removed: its text is added to the end of Personal rules, check it there.
- The Check sends your resume, vacancy, rules and sample questions to the AI providers chosen for Quick and Full.
- Interview still needs real-call acceptance.
