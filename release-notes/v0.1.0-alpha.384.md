# Me/Them Recorder 0.1.0-alpha.384

Changes since **0.1.0-alpha.372**.

## New

- Preparation has one list of answer formats that keep Quick and Full together. The exact instructions are on screen, and you can edit them and save your own format.
- Spoken points is the default answer format.
- One setting decides how Full works: it continues Quick by itself, continues it on a press, or gives a separate answer.
- Pressing Full before Quick now prepares Quick first and then continues it.
- Interview answers support concise paragraphs, with lists and examples when they help answer the question.
- Ghost has a wider, adjustable answer area and highlights key phrases for easier reading.

## Fixed

- Full no longer turns into a separate answer when Quick is slow.
- Quick keeps its answer together instead of hiding important points in expandable details.
- Clearly unfinished Quick answers are retried before being accepted as complete.
- Ghost keeps its height while a new answer is prepared and folds the previous answer.
- Interview examples favor the current project and avoid repeating earlier suggestions.
- Local transcription works again when the app is started from the source shortcut.
- Cerebras Qwen accepts the selected Interview reasoning level.

## Important

- If you had turned off starting the full answer together with the quick one, that choice carries over as a separate Full answer.
- Interview can still misread technical questions and repeat or overstate personal examples.
- Interview still needs real-call acceptance.
- Ghost still needs conferencing acceptance.
- A recording may be missing from history after transcription is cancelled.
- System-audio timing can become misaligned and change the order of transcript lines.
