# Me/Them Recorder 0.1.0-alpha.152

Cumulative alpha release from 0.1.0-alpha.126 through 0.1.0-alpha.152.
Local-first Windows recorder for microphone and system audio, transcription,
speaker-aware meeting notes, summaries, and Q&A.

## New since alpha.126

### Live transcription and Quick results

- **Selectable live method:** Call and Online room modes can use Streaming,
  Periodic chunks, or Off. Streaming keeps independent `Me` and `Them` decoder
  states and exposes committed lines plus a replaceable live tail.
- **A usable Quick transcript at Stop:** native streaming finalizes the live
  draft into a complete transcript. The post-Stop policy can ask what to do,
  keep Quick, or run the regular WhisperX/Groq improvement pass. Q&A created
  during the call follows an accepted Quick transcript.
- **Lower live latency and cleaner ordering:** per-channel update budgets,
  channel-specific sample rates and timeline offsets, stable row identities,
  pause-aware short replies, eager draining while audio is available, and
  recovery for words split between the final residual feed and finalize.
- **Safer live lifecycle:** generation fences reject late events from old
  workers, silent no-op deltas no longer re-render the drawer, recovery cancel
  keeps the draft, and partial native output is never promoted after a runtime
  failure.

### Recording reliability

- **Retry after source-audio save failure:** Stop retains the frozen in-memory
  recording and every track already written successfully. Retry repeats the
  atomic source write without restarting capture or losing the meeting.
- **Honest capture interruption handling:** an unexpected microphone or
  loopback stop is surfaced in the recorder instead of silently presenting an
  incomplete session as healthy.
- **Silent-channel filtering:** final Call and Online-room transcription skips
  raw digital silence and very low room tone, reducing invented lines from an
  unused input while preserving quiet speech and short closing replies.
- **More reliable Russian improvement:** concrete Russian evidence from Quick
  can guide Auto Improve without forcing other Cyrillic languages to Russian.
- **One admitted Start:** concurrent button, keyboard, and global-hotkey starts
  cannot create competing capture or native-streaming workers.

### Setup and configuration

- **Faster relevant startup:** the app skips unused WhisperX/Groq prewarm when
  Streaming plus Keep Quick is selected, and still warms the engine before an
  operation that needs it.
- **Clearer Settings readiness:** recognition, live transcription, speakers,
  and AI capabilities have separate status and navigation; native Q4/Q5/Q8
  model choices are identified by role and quality.
- **Loopback Custom API support:** localhost OpenAI-compatible endpoints bypass
  the system proxy.
- **More reliable release smoke:** the canonical builder now waits for Windows
  to release the installed executable before running the uninstall check.

## Native Streaming availability

The app contains the Streaming UI, coordinator, verified model catalog, and
pinned native-runtime integration. The separate `live-runtime-v1` binaries and
GGUF assets are **not published by this release**: their corpus, endurance,
hardware, crash, update, and licensing gates remain open. Periodic chunks and
the normal post-Stop WhisperX/Groq flows remain available.

## Installing

Run `MeThemRecorder-0.1.0-alpha.152-Setup.exe`. The installer is per-user (no
UAC) and defaults to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\MeThemRecorder`; the destination
page is always shown.

The Setup is the lightweight app only. Local WhisperX and Room diarization use
the separately versioned, SHA-256-verified Local Runtime (`cu121-v3` or
`cpu-v3`), which the app downloads on demand. Call/Groq-only setups need no
Local Runtime download.
