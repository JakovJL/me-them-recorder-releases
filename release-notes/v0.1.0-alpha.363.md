# Me/Them Recorder 0.1.0-alpha.363

Changes since **0.1.0-alpha.349**.

## New

- Prepare interviews by section, with longer vacancies, answer-style templates, personal rules and an English level.
- Full can build on a completed Quick answer, adding examples and detail.
- A roomier workspace puts each meeting's tools alongside the work they belong to.
- Search meeting history by transcript text and combine filters.
- Switch the AI provider while a recording is running.
- Choose interlocutors from names used in earlier meetings.
- Give unnamed meetings a title from their finished summary.
- Find speech models in one table, with separate Recording and Interview settings.
- Use the clearer per-meeting glossary editor with guided empty and search states.

## Fixed

- Set the next call's title and interlocutor before Record without renaming the previous meeting or losing input on a failed start.
- Model action buttons and Ghost switches keep their alignment.

## Important

- Existing settings and interview preparation load unchanged.
- Interview and Ghost still need full real-call and conferencing acceptance.
- A recording may be missing from history after transcription is cancelled.
- System-audio timing can become misaligned and change the order of transcript lines.
